How booking works

Every room on one screen. No phone tag.

See what’s free

Every room sits side by side on a live grid, a week at a time. Cheaper off-peak hours are marked, so you can spot a bargain at a glance.

Pick a time, see the price

Tap a slot and drag to set your session, then fine-tune by the half hour. The price builds as you go, with GST and any extras itemised.

Confirm and you’re in

Pay securely online and get a confirmation email straight away. Need to change plans? Use the link in your email to manage your booking.

Open the booking page Already booked? Manage it from the link in your confirmation email, or under My bookings on the booking page.
