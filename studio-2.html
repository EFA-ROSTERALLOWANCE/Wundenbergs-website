Studio Two.

Our smaller, more affordable studio, with the same professional standard. Built for voice, overdubs and ideas.

Enquire about Studio 2 What’s in the room
Made for

Clean, professional audio, every time.

Studio 2 is ideal for anything that needs high-quality professional audio without the full Studio 1 setup.

Much of the work here is for corporate clients: radio and TV commercials, voice overs, audiobooks and ADR. Plenty of local musicians and producers use it for pre-production, writing sessions, overdubs and re-amping too.

The sound-proof booth was professionally designed and built, and the room has just been upgraded with a Universal Audio Apollo 8 interface.
