How it works

Book it, get your code, play.

Book online

Pick a room and a start time and pay online. There are no fixed slots: start on any hour, day or night. Sessions are 4 hours minimum, then extend by the half hour.

Get your door code

Your door code arrives by email 24 hours before your session. It works from 30 minutes before you start until 30 minutes after you finish.

Play, then pack down on time

Leave yourself time to break down and load out. Power to the room switches off 15 minutes after your booking ends.

FAQ

Good to know.

When are the rooms available?

Any time. The rooms are available 24/7 and can be booked from any hour of the day. There are no set booking blocks: start whenever you like, with a minimum 4-hour session that you can extend by the half hour.

How much do the rooms cost?

Between $55 and $90 per 4-hour booking at peak times, depending on the room. Off-peak hours are 20% off:

  • Monday to Thursday: midnight to 5pm
  • Friday and Saturday: all day
  • Sunday: 5pm to midnight

Each room’s price is listed on its card above. You pay in full online when you book, and the booking page shows your exact price first.

How do I get into my room?

Our rooms use an automated coded entry system. Your door code is emailed to you 24 hours before your session. It works from 30 minutes before you start until 30 minutes after you finish.

Please be out by the time your booking finishes, leaving enough time to break down and load out. Rooms are on timers, and the power switches off 15 minutes after a booking ends.

What comes in the rooms?

Every room has a drum kit (with cymbal stands and a hi-hat stand), a PA, two or more Shure SM58 microphones with stands and XLRs, and air conditioning (except Room 6).

A snare and cymbals aren’t included, but you can hire them for $15 each when you book, along with amps and more (see gear hire). Bring your own sticks.

What’s the cancellation policy?

Cancel within 24 hours of making your booking for a full refund. You can also move your booking once, free, to another time or room, up to 48 hours before your session. Use the link in your confirmation email to do either. Otherwise, please contact us.

Damage to equipment caused by misuse will be charged to the band or person who booked. If anything is damaged or missing when you arrive, please tell us straight away.
