Music first. Anything that needs to sound right, too.

01

Recording

Full bands, solo artists, live-to-tape sessions. Tracked through vintage Neve and classic outboard into Pro Tools Ultimate or Logic Pro.

02

Mixing

Mixed on the 5088 with a rack of industry-standard compressors and EQs, for warmth and depth you can hear.

03

Production

From pre-production and arrangement through to finished masters, with an award-winning engineer and producer in the chair.

04

Treated rooms, great mics and an engineer who knows the spoken word. Clean, consistent audio, ready to publish.

About WRS

Serious gear. A room that feels like home.

We’ve won the South Australian Music Award for Studio of the Year three years running, and Lewis Wundenberg took home Engineer/Producer of the Year in 2023.

But the awards aren’t why people come back. It’s the homely atmosphere, well-maintained equipment, skilled engineers and hours that work around real life. We record, produce and mix music, and we bring the same care to podcasts, audiobooks and voice overs for film, TV and online.
