The heart of the room

A Neve console with a story.

Studio 1 is built around a 16-channel Rupert Neve Designs 5088, paired with a 12-channel 1980s AMS Neve V-Series sidecar.

The sidecar started life at the BBC before Australian rock legend Billy Thorpe bought it for his home studio. Add four AMS Neve 4081 preamps, based on the classic 1081, and there are 16 more Neve pres on tap.

Around it sits a rack of industry standards: Neve 33609, LA-2A, four 1176s, Neve 1272s, Avalon 737SP and four Distressors. All of it adds silky harmonics and real warmth before the sound reaches our high-end converters.
